Last Updated: September 21 2026

Welcome to Word Ladder. We at Mbread Studio respect your privacy and we are committed to protecting it. This document explains what information we collect when you use our mobile application and how we use it.

We do not collect any personal information directly from you. The game saves your progress, such as your current level and high scores, locally on your device. We do not transmit this data to any external servers.

3. Third Party Services

Our game uses Google AdMob to show advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and use certain information about your device, such as your internet protocol address and advertising identifier, to serve relevant advertisements. You can learn more about how Google handles this data by reading the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal information at all, we do not collect data from children or adults of any age. The game is completely safe for everyone.
